Privacy policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

This page explains what information Grade Book collects, why, and who else handles it. Grade Book has no ads and no tracking or analytics tools, and we don't sell your information.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, your email address, and your password. Passwords are stored in scrambled (hashed) form, so we can't read them.
  • Your grades: the classes, scores, dates, and settings you enter.
  • Trial and payment status: whether your free trial is active or cancelled, when it ends, whether you've paid, and the order and subscription numbers from Lemon Squeezy. We never see or store your card number.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show your grades on any device you use.
  • To know whether your free trial is active or you've paid.
  • To send emails you need, like confirming your email address or resetting your password. We don't send marketing emails.

Who can see your grades

Only you can see your grades in Grade Book. The person who runs Grade Book can access the database to fix problems, but won't look at your grades unless you ask for help with them.

Services that handle your information

  • Supabase stores your account and grades on servers in the United States.
  • Lemon Squeezy runs checkout and payments. They collect your name, email, card, and billing address to charge you, under their privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like most web hosts, it may log your IP address when you visit.
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs deliver code that Grade Book runs in your browser. They can see your IP address when your browser downloads it.

Stored on your device

Grade Book saves a copy of your grades and your sign-in in your browser's local storage, so the page loads quickly and keeps you signed in. Signing out removes them from that browser, unless some changes haven't saved to your account yet.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • We keep your information while you have an account.
  • Delete all grades in Settings erases your grades.
  • Delete account in Settings deletes your account and all your grades right away. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on the account, and we'll delete it within 30 days.
  • Lemon Squeezy keeps its own payment records, as tax law requires.

Children

Grade Book is not meant for children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has made an account, we'll delete it. If you think this has happened, email us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll also tell you in Grade Book or by email.
